## IDEAL AFFILIATE PROFILES

### Tier 1: Micro-Creators (1K-10K followers)
- **Why**: Highest engagement rates (5-15%), loyal audiences, affordable
- **Target**: 20-30 micro-creators in first 30 days
- **Outreach method**: Direct DM + gifted product
- **Expected output**: 3-5 sales each = 60-150 total sales

### Tier 2: Mid-Tier Creators (10K-100K followers)
- **Why**: Meaningful reach with decent engagement (3-8%)
- **Target**: 5-10 mid-tier creators in first 30 days
- **Outreach method**: Email + product gift + paid post offer
- **Expected output**: 20-50 sales each = 100-500 total sales

### Tier 3: Niche Authorities
- **Who**: Business coaches, fashion bloggers, lifestyle creators
- **Why**: Their recommendation = instant credibility
- **Target**: 2-3 strategic partnerships
- **Outreach method**: Personalized proposal + revenue share
- **Expected output**: 50-200 sales each

## TIKTOK SHOP AFFILIATE STRATEGY

### Setting Up Open Collaboration:
1. List all boutique products on TikTok Shop
2. Set Open Collaboration rate at **18%** (competitive for fashion)
3. Enable Targeted Collaboration for top creators at **25%**
4. Send free samples to 10 creators/week through Shop's affiliate hub

### Optimizing for Affiliate Discovery:
- Use high-quality product images (white background + lifestyle)
- Write detailed product descriptions with keywords
- Set competitive commission (below 8% = invisible in marketplace)
- Respond to affiliate inquiries within 24 hours

### Affiliate Content Guidelines:
Provide affiliates with:
- [ ] Brand style guide (colors, fonts, do's and don'ts)
- [ ] 5-10 pre-written captions they can customize
- [ ] Product photography they can use
- [ ] Talking points for each product
- [ ] Example content that performed well

## COLLABORATION TYPES BEYOND AFFILIATES

### 1. Live Takeovers
Invite a creator to co-host your TikTok Live. Split the audience exposure. Both promote it to their followers.

### 2. Product Collabs
Co-create a limited-edition boutique item or digital product with a creator. Split revenue 50/50. Both audiences buy.

### 3. Bundle Partnerships
"My boutique piece + their product = exclusive bundle." Cross-sell to both audiences.

### 4. Podcast/Interview Circuit
Get on 2-3 podcasts in the business/entrepreneur space. Free exposure. Immediate authority boost.

### 5. Email List Swaps
Find a creator with a similar-sized list. Each promote the other's lead magnet to their list. Both lists grow.

## AFFILIATE TRACKING & MANAGEMENT

### Tools:
- **TikTok Shop**: Built-in affiliate tracking
- **Shopify Collabs** or **Refersion**: For boutique affiliate tracking
- **Stan Store**: Built-in affiliate link generation for digital products

### Monthly Affiliate Operations:
- **Week 1**: Recruit 5-10 new affiliates
- **Week 2**: Check in with existing affiliates — share new products, offers
- **Week 3**: Analyze top performers — offer tier upgrades
- **Week 4**: Pay commissions, send thank-you notes, plan next month

| Revenue from affiliates | $1,000-3,000 | $5,000-10,000 |
