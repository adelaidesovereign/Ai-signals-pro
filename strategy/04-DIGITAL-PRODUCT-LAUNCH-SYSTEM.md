## PHASE 1: SEED (Days 1-14)

### Goals:
- Build email list to 500-1,000 subscribers minimum
- Create 10+ pieces of pre-launch content
- Establish authority on the topic
- Generate waitlist of 200+ interested buyers

### Tactics:
1. **Lead magnet launch**: "5 Steps to Your First $1K Online" (free PDF)
2. **Daily TikTok content**: 3 videos/day hitting pain points the course solves
3. **TikTok Lives**: 2x daily, teaching snippets from the course
4. **Instagram Stories**: Behind-the-scenes of course creation
5. **Email sequence**: Welcome → Value → Value → Story → Waitlist invite

## PHASE 2: LAUNCH (Days 15-21)

### Launch Week Email Sequence (7 emails):

**Email 6 — Cart Open (Day 15)**
Subject: "Doors are OPEN. Build Your Empire is live."
- Full sales pitch with module breakdown
- Founding member price + bonus stack
- 48-hour early access bonus for email list
- CTA: Enroll Now

**Email 7 — Social Proof (Day 16)**
Subject: "47 women enrolled in the first 6 hours"
- Share enrollment numbers
- Student excitement screenshots
- Emphasize momentum + community
- CTA: Join them

**Email 8 — Objection Crusher (Day 17)**
Subject: "You're thinking about it. Let me help."
- Address top 3 objections: time, money, "will this work for me?"
- Use FAQ format
- End with risk-reversal
- CTA: Your future self will thank you

**Email 9 — Testimonial Deep Dive (Day 18)**
Subject: "She quit her 9-5 in 90 days"
- Full case study of a beta student
- Before/after transformation
- Specific revenue numbers
- CTA: This could be your story

**Email 10 — Bonus Reminder (Day 19)**
Subject: "These bonuses expire in 48 hours"
- Re-list the bonus stack with values
- Emphasize the live coaching call (limited spots)
- Scarcity is REAL — the call can't hold unlimited people
- CTA: Don't miss this

**Email 11 — Last Day (Day 20)**
Subject: "Final call. Cart closes at midnight."
- Emotional + urgent
- "A year from now, you'll wish you started today"
- Recap everything they get
- Last chance for founding price
- CTA: This is it

**Email 12 — Cart Closed / Waitlist (Day 21)**
Subject: "Doors are closed. But..."
- Thank buyers, welcome them
- For non-buyers: "The price goes up. But you can join the waitlist for next round."
- No shame, no pressure — just facts
- CTA: Join the waitlist

## PHASE 3: EVERGREEN TRANSITION (Days 22-30)

### Automated Evergreen Funnel:
```
New subscriber → Lead magnet → Welcome sequence (5 emails) 
→ Waitlist/sales page → Automated webinar replay 
→ 48-hour urgency sequence → Purchase or nurture loop
```

### Nurture Loop (non-buyers):
- Weekly value email every Tuesday
- Monthly "doors open" flash launch (3-day windows)
- Continuous TikTok content driving to lead magnet

## LAUNCH DAY TIKTOK LIVE SCRIPT

### "Build Your Empire Launch Party" (2-hour event)

**0:00-0:15** — Hype & Welcome
> "TODAY IS THE DAY. If you've been following along, you know what this is. Build Your Empire is officially LIVE. Stay on this live because I'm going to walk you through everything — and there's a live-only bonus that expires when I end this stream."

**0:15-0:45** — Module Walkthrough
> Walk through each module with screen shares or slides. For each module: "Here's what you'll learn, here's WHY it matters, and here's a quick win from inside the module."

**0:45-1:00** — Student Spotlight
> Bring a beta student on (or read their message). Let them share their experience in their own words.

**1:00-1:15** — Bonus Stack Reveal
> "Now here's what makes this insane for founding members..." List every bonus, show the value stack, let it sink in.

**1:15-1:30** — Live Q&A
> Answer every question. Address objections in real time. This is where trust converts to transactions.

**1:30-1:45** — Price Reveal + Live-Only Bonus
> "The investment is $297. But for everyone on this live right now — I'm adding a bonus that's NOT on the sales page. [Reveal live-only bonus]. Drop 'EMPIRE' in the chat to get the link."

**1:45-2:00** — Final Push + Celebration
> Celebrate live enrollments as they come in. Shout out names. Create a party atmosphere. End with: "This is just the beginning. See you inside."
